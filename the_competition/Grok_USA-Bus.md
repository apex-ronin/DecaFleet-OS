## Executive Summary & Core Mechanics

### Lead Offer: Sub Radar (Contrarian)
- **Thesis:** A general contractor pays $149 for a 48-hour check of up to 40 subs against the public state licensing board, then $399/month to have it rechecked every weekday. You ping them only when a license, bond, or workers-comp flag changes.
- **Unfair Advantage:** First dollar does not need Google, Amazon, OnlyFans, or anyone's login credentials. Completely public government registries (e.g., CSLB, TDLR, DBPR). Zero anti-bot ban risk.

### Phased Roadmap:
1. **Day 1–30:** Sub Radar ($149 audit → $399/mo) — reaches $12k–$16k MRR inside 20 hours/week.
2. **Day 90:** Trade Map Desk ($449/mo) — sold to the exact same contractors to manage their Google Business Profiles and local trade listings.
3. **Month 6:** Seat Sniper ($400/mo) — SaaS license optimization for 8–40 person firms (introduced only after client trust and admin approval habits are established).

### Ruthless Kills:
- **Parcel Watch (Killed):** Certain counties explicitly ban commercial resale of public tax records; weak monthly recurring pain point.
- **Amazon Listings & Price Memos (Shelf Copy / Shelf Price) (Parked):** Aggressive Amazon scraping risks getting the shared machine/IP blocked, halting the entire 10-bot fleet.
- **Upwork Bidding, Review-Request Tools, Deal Registration (Killed):** Bots are either decorative or violate platform policies, risking client bans.
